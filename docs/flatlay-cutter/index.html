Fast flatlay-reference cutter

The flatlay defines the product. The cutout comes from the model-worn photo.

90 ms to a mask · 329 ms from file to saved cutout on RTX 5090. Warm, batch one; cached flatlay preparation.

33 worn images, each timed three times. File-to-output median 273 ms; p95 646 ms. Model loading, shot selection and one-time flatlay preparation are excluded.

Mean mask overlap with the previous cutter: 99.95%. All four wrong-reference probes reject. This measures agreement on this sample, not ground-truth accuracy or general identity verification.

What changed?

Product types and colours are cached from the flatlay. Each worn image runs one SAM3 image encoder, cached text prompts and a coarse colour mismatch check. There are no per-image language-model calls. Long descriptions and their extra mask proposals have been removed. Masks retain disconnected pieces and openings.

A colour check cannot resolve every same-colour styling ambiguity. These 33 examples are a development sample; broader quality is unverified. Earlier cutter with separate visual checks.
