How the eight VTON models are trained

Author-reported recipes, conditioning paths, and the exact releases used in our comparison. Updated 29 September 2026. Training dimensions are height × width.

Our benchmark is inference only: 128 shared paired VITON-HD test cases, eight released checkpoints, 1,024 outputs at 1024×768 on H200. No model was retrained for this comparison.

We used a common OOTD-derived inpainting mask with remapped dataset parsing labels and supplied pose coordinates, while retaining each model’s required conditioning. Optional final RGB compositing was disabled. This is a controlled comparison with adapted inputs, not an exact reproduction of every paper’s evaluation.

Molmo localizes components; frozen DINOv2 and our trained regional experts score generated versus GT features at shared GT-defined locations. Lower normalized expert L2 distance is better. These scores are not percentages, and automatic localization errors can affect them.

Recipe gaps

The DeCo paper documents a 512-resolution recipe, while our benchmark uses its released 1024 checkpoint. CatVTON-FLUX alpha has no exact checkpoint training receipt in the cited sources, and its public script’s parameter selection differs from the README’s full-fine-tuning description. FastFit’s paper describes its schedule and architecture but leaves the complete loss and trainable-parameter selection unspecified. Those gaps are recorded rather than filled with assumptions.
